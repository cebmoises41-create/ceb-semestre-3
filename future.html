💡 Grammar Tip: Usa will para decisiones espontáneas y going to para planes ya decididos.

Dialogue 1 — Planning a weekend trip

A: Hey! Do you have any plans for the weekend? I visit my grandparents.
B: That sounds nice! I stay home and relax.
A: Look at the sky, it's getting dark. I think it rain soon.
B: You're right. I take an umbrella just in case.
A: Good idea. My parents pick me up at 5 PM.
B: I call you later tonight.
A: Okay. We watch a movie together next week.
B: Perfect! I buy the tickets.
A: Thanks. What time we meet?
B: Let's meet at 7. I promise I not be late!
A: Look at that car! It crash!
B: No, don't worry. The driver stop in time.
A: I hope so. I go home now. See you next week!
B: Wait! I walk with you to the bus stop.
A: Thanks! Bye!

Dialogue 2 — At the restaurant

A: Great choice. And to drink?
B: I try the lemonade. I heard it's good.
A: Yes, it is. We bring it right away.
B: Thank you. By the way, is it going to be busy tonight?
A: Yes, there's a big party later. But don't worry, I find you a quiet table.
B: That's very kind. My wife join me in a few minutes.
A: No problem. I bring an extra menu for her.
B: Thanks. I think we enjoy this dinner very much.
A: I'm sure you will. Here is your lemonade. Enjoy!

Dialogue 3 — At the doctor's office

A: Good morning, doctor. I feel terrible.
B: Good morning. What's wrong? I check your temperature.
A: I have a headache and a sore throat.
B: Okay. I prescribe you some medicine.
A: Do I need to stay home?
B: Yes. You rest for at least two days.
A: I have a lot of work. I call my boss right now.
B: Good idea. I write a note for your boss.
A: Thank you, doctor. I take the medicine every 8 hours.
B: Exactly. And drink lots of water. You feel better soon.
A: I hope so. Goodbye!

Dialogue 4 — At the airport
