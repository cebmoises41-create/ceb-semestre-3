Future: Will & Going To

📖 Explanation

WILL: Usamos will para decisiones espontáneas, promesas o predicciones sin pruebas.
Example: I will help you.

GOING TO: Usamos going to para planes ya decididos o predicciones basadas en lo que vemos.
Example: Look at those clouds! It is going to rain.
