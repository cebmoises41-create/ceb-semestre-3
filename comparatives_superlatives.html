📊 Comparatives - Explanation

🔹 Regla 1: Adjetivos cortos (1 sílaba)

Adjetivo + -er + than

→ tall → taller than

→ fast → faster than

→ big → bigger than (doble consonante)

🔹 Regla 2: Adjetivos de 2 sílabas terminados en -y

-y → -ier + than

→ happy → happier than

→ busy → busier than

🔹 Regla 3: Adjetivos largos (2+ sílabas)

more + adjetivo + than

→ expensive → more expensive than

→ beautiful → more beautiful than

🔹 Regla 4: Irregulares

→ good → better than

→ bad → worse than

→ far → farther than

🔹 Estructura de la oración

Sujeto + verbo + comparativo + than + objeto

→ A car is cheaper than a plane.

→ She is taller than her brother.

⚠️ Recuerda:

🔹 Adjetivos cortos → +er

🔹 Adjetivos largos → more +

🔹 Siempre usa than para comparar
