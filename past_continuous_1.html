📘 Past Continuous

🔹 What is it?

It is used for actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.

Formula: Subject + was/were + Verb (-ing)

✅ I was studying at 8 PM.
✅ They were playing soccer.
❌ He wasn't sleeping.
❓ Were you reading?

🔹 When to use WAS vs WERE?

WAS: I, He, She, It.

WERE: You, We, They.
