📖 Present Perfect - Explanation

🔹 Usos del Present Perfect

  • Experiencias pasadas (sin tiempo específico)
    → I have visited Paris.
  • Acciones que empezaron en el pasado y continúan
    → She has lived here for 5 years.
  • Acciones recientes con resultado presente
    → I have lost my keys. (No puedo entrar)

🔹 Estructura

✅ Afirmativo: Sujeto + have/has + past participle

→ I have eaten.

→ She has gone.

❌ Negativo: Sujeto + have/has + not + past participle

→ I have not eaten. / I haven't eaten.

→ She has not gone. / She hasn't gone.

❓ Interrogativo: Have/Has + sujeto + past participle?

→ Have you eaten?

→ Has she gone?

🔹 Past Participle

  • Verbos regulares: +ed (walked, played, studied)
  • Verbos irregulares: 3ª columna (eaten, gone, seen, written)

🔹 Palabras clave

  • ever → ¿alguna vez?
  • never → nunca
  • already → ya
  • yet → todavía / ya (negativas y preguntas)
  • just → acabar de
  • for → durante (periodo de tiempo)
  • since → desde (punto en el tiempo)

⚠️ Regla importante:

El verbo auxiliar HAVE/HAS se usa con el PAST PARTICIPLE.

✅ I have seen that movie.

❌ I have saw that movie.
