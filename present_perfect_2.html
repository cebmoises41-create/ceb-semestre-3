📖 Present Perfect - Negative

🔹 Estructura

Sujeto + have/has + not + past participle

🔹 Contracciones

  • have not → haven't
  • has not → hasn't

🔹 Ejemplos

  • ✅ I have not eaten breakfast. / I haven't eaten breakfast.
  • ✅ She has not finished yet. / She hasn't finished yet.
  • ✅ They have not arrived. / They haven't arrived.
  • ✅ He has not called me. / He hasn't called me.

🔹 Usos

  • Decir que algo NO ha ocurrido
  • Con "yet" al final de la oración
    → I haven't seen that movie yet.
  • Con "never" para decir que nunca ha pasado
    → I have never been to Paris.

⚠️ Regla importante:

El verbo auxiliar HAVE/HAS se usa con NOT + PAST PARTICIPLE.

✅ I haven't seen that movie.

❌ I don't have seen that movie.

📖 Present Perfect - Interrogative

🔹 Estructura

Have/Has + sujeto + past participle?

🔹 Ejemplos

  • ✅ Have you eaten breakfast?
  • ✅ Has she finished her work?
  • ✅ Have they arrived yet?
  • ✅ Has he called you?

🔹 Usos

  • Preguntar si algo ha ocurrido
  • Con "ever" para preguntar sobre experiencias
    → Have you ever been to Paris?
  • Con "yet" para preguntar si algo ya pasó
    → Has she called you yet?

⚠️ Regla importante:

El verbo auxiliar HAVE/HAS va al PRINCIPIO de la pregunta.

✅ Have you seen that movie?

❌ You have seen that movie?
