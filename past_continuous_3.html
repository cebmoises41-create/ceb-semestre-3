📖 Reading: A Bad Day for Alex

Yesterday was a terrible day for Alex. At 8 AM, he was riding his bike to work when it started to rain heavily. He didn't have an umbrella! While he was running to find shelter, he dropped his phone in a puddle. Later, at 10 AM, he was sitting in a café feeling sad when a stranger bought him a hot coffee. That made him smile!

💬 Dialogue: At the Police Station

Officer: What were you doing at 9 PM last night?
Alex: I was walking my dog in the park.
Officer: Did you see anything strange?
Alex: Yes! While I was walking, a man ran past me very fast!
Officer: What was he wearing?
Alex: He was wearing a black jacket and he was carrying a red bag.
