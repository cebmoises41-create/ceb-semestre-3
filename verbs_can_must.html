📖 CAN - Explanation

🔹 Usos de CAN

  • Habilidad: I can swim. (Sé nadar)
  • Permiso: Can I go to the bathroom? (¿Puedo ir al baño?)
  • Posibilidad: It can rain today. (Puede llover hoy)

🔹 Estructura

✅ Afirmativo: Sujeto + can + verbo infinitivo

→ I can play soccer.

→ She can sing beautifully.

❌ Negativo: Sujeto + cannot / can't + verbo infinitivo

→ I cannot swim. / I can't swim.

→ He can't drive.

❓ Interrogativo: Can + sujeto + verbo infinitivo?

→ Can you help me?

→ Can she speak French?

⚠️ Regla importante:

El verbo después de CAN siempre va en INFINITIVO (sin "to").

✅ I can sing.

❌ I can to sing.

📖 MUST - Explanation

🔹 Usos de MUST

  • Obligación: You must wear a seatbelt. (Debes usar cinturón)
  • Prohibición: You must not smoke here. (No debes fumar aquí)

🔹 Estructura

✅ Afirmativo: Sujeto + must + verbo infinitivo

→ You must study for the exam.

→ We must wear a uniform.

❌ Negativo: Sujeto + must not / mustn't + verbo infinitivo

→ You must not run in the hallways.

→ We mustn't eat in class.

❓ Interrogativo: Must + sujeto + verbo infinitivo?

→ Must I wear a uniform?

→ Must we arrive early?

⚠️ Regla importante:

El verbo después de MUST siempre va en INFINITIVO (sin "to").

✅ You must listen to the teacher.

❌ You must to listen.
